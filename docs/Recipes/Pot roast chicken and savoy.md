Pot Roast Chicken & Savoy
Server 4 
prep: 20 cooking: 35

### Ingredients:
-  4 Chicken breast , skin on, on the bone
- 1 medium savoy cabbage
- 1 medium  onion
- 8 slices pancetta
- 2 Cloves of garlic
- 1 tbs of dried greek sage leaves
- 2 Bay leaves
- 2 large glasses of dry white wine

### Destructions:
- Pre-heat the oven to 200°C/gas 6/400°F.
- Heat 2tbs of olive oil on a large casserole dish, add the chicken and cook for a few minutes on each side until it starts to brown, set aside.
- Return the casserole dish to heat, add butter and a splash of olive oil, once the butter has melted add the onion and garlic and cook for 4-5 minutes.
- Add the pancetta, sage, salt and pepper and cook for a further 2-3 minutes.
-  Add the bay leaves, white wine, mix well and then return the chicken to the dish.
- Based well and place in the oven for 30 -35 minutes.
- While the chicken is cooking core the Cabbage, slice into thin strips and boil for 3-4 minutes.
- Once cooked remove the chicken from the casserole, drain the cabbage and mix into the remain juice.

Server the chicken on a bed of the cabbage, goes well with Parmesan Parsnips.
