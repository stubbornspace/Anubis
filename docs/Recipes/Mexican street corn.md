Mexican street corn

Elote (Mexican street corn) or Esquites (off-the-cob version) combines grilled corn with a tangy sauce, the kick from Tajín, and the salty goodness of cotija cheese!

Cooking time: 20 minutes

Ingredients

4 Ears of corn, husks removed
1/4 Cup mayonnaise
1/4 Cup sour cream
1/2 Cup crumbled cotija cheese
2 Tsp Tajin (or chili powder)
2 lime
Fresh cilantro
Salt &amp; Pepper to taste
Destructions

**Heat your grill to medium-high. Place the corn directly on the grill grates and cook, turning occasionally, until charred and tender, about 10–12 minutes.**

**While the corn is grilling, mix together mayonnaise, sour cream, the juice of half a lime, 1 teaspoon of Tajín, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl.**

**Once the corn is done, remove it from the grill. Generously brush or spoon the sauce over each ear of corn. Top with crumbled cotija cheese, additional Tajín, and chopped cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.**

**Alternatively, to remove the kernels from the cob, stand each ear of corn upright on a cutting board. Use a sharp knife to carefully slice down the sides of the cob, cutting off the kernels. Mix the kernels with the sauce, cotija cheese, extra Tajín, and cilantro. Serve this flavorful mixture as a side dish**
